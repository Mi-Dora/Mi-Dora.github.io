--- title: "RF-Signal-based Human Pose Estimation" excerpt: "

2020

  • Applied Radio Frequency (RF) signals for human pose estimation; succeeded in operation in dark light and passing through obstacles while being more sensitive to biological characteristics.
" collection: projects ---
  • Did camera calibration and RF device embedded software to construct a synchronous collection system to collect a dataset of image and RF signal with more than 10,000 RF signals and corresponding images.
  • Deployed OpenPose and AlphaPose for automatic annotation of optical images; developed the visual annotation tool to refine the results of the automatic annotation.
  • Trained the network model based on Cross-Model Supervision method using PyTorch; reached super resolution estimation result on test set; succeeded to do pose estimation through curtain without any training.
